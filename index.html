How a shoot works

Built around your operations, so the line keeps running.

  1. Brief

    You share what the photos are for and where they will be used. A written estimate follows.

  2. Site check

    We agree on areas, timing and anything confidential. I follow your safety briefing and PPE rules.

  3. Shoot

    On location, in working conditions and low light, without stopping a machine.

  4. Delivery

    Culled, colour-corrected high-resolution files plus web-ready versions, delivered digitally.

Usage and pricing

No rigid packages. You pay for what the project actually needs.

Licensing in writing

Usage rights for web, print or full commercial use are agreed before the shoot. No surprises later.

Revisions included

Changes are handled on request so the final set fits your requirements.

Confidential areas respected

Nothing you mark as restricted is photographed or published.
